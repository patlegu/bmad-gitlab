## What the fourth audit found, and what was done (0.1.6)

- **Fixed:** the fence parser follows CommonMark (length of the fence, info strings, indentation, a fence inside
  a line of text); a key or heading number has 1 to 6 ASCII digits; a key holding `-->` is refused; the
  summary of a run counts the issues closed as they were created apart from the closings of updates;
  `gl_setup` follows a link to its configuration, writes a character outside the BMP and DEL correctly, and
  creates no folder when it refuses; the exit codes of the three entry points are pinned by a test; the
  documentation of the eight public data classes, of `--allow-problems` and of the README rules.
- **Tests added** for what the audit measured as surviving: the separator of labels, the content type and the
  body keys sent to `glab`, the arguments and the limit of the real runner, a summary of an update that only
  adds or only removes labels, a list of a board that holds something that is not a label, several prefixes
  and sprints in the setup, the unreadable hook, the frozen data classes.

## Open, and accepted

- The `SPRINT=TEXT` rule is written in `cli.py` and `gl_setup.py`; the launcher only checks the `=`. The two
  scripts of the skill are standalone on purpose and cannot import the engine before it is on the path.
- `model.py` holds three things (status file, epics file, items); `problems` is an output parameter of three
  small functions; `_Sprint.project` holds a sprint name; the vocabulary of statuses is in three places;
  `update_issue` re-checks at run time what the plan types as a `Literal`; a heading number of seven digits or
  more is not read as a heading.
- Not measured: whether GitLab compares label names without regard to case (the planner compares them with it).

The CQI waiver of the private repository stays: no audit has recorded 9,0.
